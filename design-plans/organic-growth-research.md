# HouseSpace: zero-budget organic growth research

Researched 10 September 2026. This is a policy-safe acquisition plan for HouseSpace, a browser-based collaborative home-design and 2D-plan-to-3D product. It deliberately excludes bought traffic, engagement incentives, bulk outreach, vote manipulation, fake accounts, and thin AI-generated SEO pages.

## The practical growth loop

1. **Make a result worth sharing.** Give every completed project an optional public, indexable case-study page: the original plan (with permission), an interactive or video walkthrough, the design decisions, dimensions/style constraints, and a clear “remix this space” call to action. The asset is useful on its own, not a teaser page.
2. **Turn each result into one helpful answer and one search page.** Publish only where HouseSpace has first-hand evidence: e.g. a before/after reconstruction of a small 3BHK, how circulation changed a layout, or an editable template for a particular constraint. Show the work, disclose the maker relationship, and link only where the community allows it.
3. **Invite contribution, not votes.** Ask for a specific design critique, an anonymized plan to reconstruct, or a template submission. Credit contributors and turn their approved work into the next reusable example. This produces a library of real design evidence and a reason to share.
4. **Convert with a low-friction collaboration moment.** The public page should let a visitor open the project, duplicate it, or send it to another decision-maker. Track these actions as the growth metric, not raw views.

This loop is a proposed product-and-content strategy, not a claim about any platform algorithm.

## Product Hunt: use the current listing safely

HouseSpace already has a Product Hunt page. Do **not** create a duplicate launch or ask for upvotes. Product Hunt says a relaunch normally requires at least six months plus a significant product update; minor UI or pricing changes do not qualify. If a material new capability ships (for example, genuinely usable blueprint import or collaborative AI design), request/prepare a relaunch with that feature as the story. [Product Hunt relaunch policy](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/484934-can-i-relaunch-my-product)

On a future launch day, share the post organically with relevant communities where the maker is already active, ask for feedback/comments rather than upvotes, and reply to comments. Product Hunt prohibits mass messages, incentives, coordinated voting, paying hunters/promoters, and asking specifically for upvotes; it may remove or ban violators. [Product Hunt sharing rules](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/2690626-how-do-i-share-my-post), [launch guidance](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch)

## A 30-day operating cadence

**Week 1 — foundation.** Define a single audience/problem wedge (for example: apartment owners turning an existing plan into an editable 3D layout). Produce two excellent, consented public project stories and one template. Ensure each has a clear title, explanatory copy, descriptive image alt text, and a working open/duplicate route.

**Weeks 2–3 — evidence, not promotion.** Publish one project story or template per week. Make 3–5 useful contributions per week across communities the founder actually participates in; adapt each to the discussion and community rules. Capture recurring questions as candidates for the next case study. Invite a limited number of people to submit a plan or try a template; never automate DMs or ask for votes.

**Week 4 — double down.** Review which story/page brings project opens, duplicates, shares, and completed projects. Improve the strongest asset and build the next one around the same real problem. Keep a short public changelog of user-informed improvements and use a meaningful release—not a cosmetic refresh—as the potential future Product Hunt relaunch narrative.

### Search setup checklist

- Give every intended-to-rank story/template a unique, concise title and meta description; render the important content in accessible HTML rather than only inside a canvas.
- Create and submit a sitemap containing canonical, full URLs through Search Console. A sitemap helps Google discover the URLs you want indexed but does not guarantee indexing. [Google sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap)
- Add structured data only when it represents visible, accurate page content. Valid markup can make a page eligible for enhanced results, but Google does not guarantee an enhanced result. [Google structured-data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies)

## Scoreboard

Use a weekly sheet or dashboard with these definitions:

- **Qualified organic visits:** visitors to a case study/template from unpaid search, direct sharing, or allowed community links.
- **Activation rate:** qualified visitors who open or duplicate a project / qualified organic visits.
- **Collaboration rate:** activated visitors who share a project or invite a collaborator / activated visitors.
- **Return contribution rate:** users who submit feedback, a design result, or a template within 30 days / activated users.
- **Content quality:** number of project stories/templates based on real, permissioned work—not number of URLs published.

Avoid optimizing for Product Hunt rank, impressions alone, or a volume of generic pages. These are easy to inflate and weak evidence of a durable user base.

## Compliance guardrails

- No bots, purchased traffic, engagement rings, giveaways for votes, fake identities, bulk DMs, or coordinated voting. Product Hunt and Reddit both explicitly prohibit forms of inauthentic engagement; Product Hunt can remove listings/accounts. [Product Hunt launch guidance](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/before-launch), [Reddit Spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)
- No keyword stuffing, copied pages, or scaled AI content whose purpose is ranking rather than usefulness. Google says these practices can result in lower ranking or removal from Search. [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
- Get clear permission before publishing a user’s floor plan, home photos, address, or recognizable details; anonymize by default. This is product judgment and should be reflected in the consent flow.

## Open decisions before execution

1. Which one user segment and use case will HouseSpace own first?
2. Can a visitor currently open, duplicate, and share a project without an account or with a very low-friction account step?
3. What consent, redaction, and deletion controls exist for submitted plans and home imagery?
